Real estate website

01 · The problem

First-time buyers are anxious and research-heavy, and most agent sites ask them to book a call before giving them anything useful. My older site also had SSL and maintenance problems, and a browser security warning loses a visitor before they read a word.

02 · What I did

  • Relaunched the site and fixed the SSL and browser issues.

  • Built booking into the site itself (no Calendly-style widget): it checks calendar availability, sends confirmations and creates the Google Meet link.
  • Created the custom graphics and marketing content.

02

Lead qualification automation

Questionnaire to booked call · with a live demo Apps ScriptLead scoringEmail automation

01 · The problem

Buyer leads used to arrive as a vague "contact me" message, and every follow-up meant writing a reply by hand. I wanted every buyer to get real value straight away, and my follow-up time to go to the people ready to act.

02 · What I did

  • A short mobile questionnaire collects budget, savings, timeline, property type, area and pre-approval status.
  • Google Apps Script cleans each submission and runs the calculations.
  • The script fills a branded template with the buyer's own numbers and exports a personalized Buyer Blueprint PDF.
  • Each lead gets an internal status (hot, warm, cold or needs work) based on timeline, finances and readiness.
  • The Blueprint is emailed automatically, and the buyer can book a call on the site without waiting on me.
  • Submissions and bookings are logged in separate Google Sheets, a lightweight CRM.

How I used AI

ChatGPT, Claude and Google AI Studio helped me write the Blueprint copy and develop the script logic. I tested the output and checked it myself.

    Castoreum Canada

    castoreumcanada.ca · B2B export website     B2B contentBilingual EN/FRSEO structure

    01 · The problem

    Solitaire Globals has specialized in beaver castoreum since 2009 and wanted a stronger online presence to reach buyers abroad. Their buyers (fragrance houses, tincture makers, traders) need to know grades, minimum order, documents and legality before they inquire, and there's no online checkout, so the goal is a qualified quote request.

    02 · What I did

    • Spec strip in the hero: grades, 50 kg minimum, species, origin and worldwide shipping, answered without scrolling.
    • Product information architecture: one card per grade with a "best for" use case, plus pages on grades, pricing and "what is castoreum".
    • French content for France, the priority market (Grasse and Paris perfumery).
    • Trust signals: real warehouse photos, a four-step ordering process, an export-route globe and a FAQ covering CITES and export documents.
    • Low-friction inquiries: a pre-written quote message and a WhatsApp link with a ready-made greeting.

    03 · Measuring it

    Search Console and GA4 are tracking the English and French pages. Next: GA4 events that compare quote-form sends with WhatsApp clicks. No rankings, traffic, leads or sales are claimed for this project.
